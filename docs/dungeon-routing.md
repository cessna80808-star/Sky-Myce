# Dungeon routing

Dungeon Routing uses the dungeon map, loaded client collision geometry and an optimized target order to guide you through starred mobs and onward toward Blood Room. It starts in the dungeon waiting room as soon as the floor and a useful map connection are known, before Mort's start announcement. Starting the run preserves warmed geometry and onward paths. Confirmed Wither doors are intermediate crossings in a continuous room/door route ending at Blood, including closed gates. Routes and mob markers hide as soon as Blood opens, even while outside Blood; they also hide without a useful connection or in the boss phase. Mages keep the actual Blood doorway highlighted for the run, before and after opening, except while physically inside Blood. Leaving Blood restores that highlight, with no timer. The doorway and Blood room segments are cached until the run resets, so delayed current-room metadata cannot keep the highlight visible inside Blood. Other classes receive no post-opening highlight. The reminder respects Show Blood guidance, uses Noamm's 3×4×3 doorway bounds, and hides on death, in the boss phase or when the feature is disabled. Small door-approach waypoint boxes are removed. It only draws guidance; it never moves, attacks, opens doors or sends gameplay packets.

**No room catalog is required for automatic routing.** Version 2.4.14 incorrectly depended on a manually authored catalog and therefore displayed nothing on a normal installation. Version 2.4.15 starts automatic room detection whenever no authored template matches. `DungeonAPI.roomId` comes from scoreboard metadata and must not be treated as a room-layout identifier.

## Automatic routing

Enable **Dungeon Routing** under **Dungeons → Routes** and enter the dungeon waiting room. NoammAddons' scanned topology can supply pre-start connections; the native reader also uses the dungeon's filled map in its normal ninth hotbar slot once available. `/sm route status` reports the current room, observed targets and scan status without requiring Developer Mode. The native map reader calibrates the green entrance against the fixed dungeon grid and floor padding, recognizes joined room segments and reads server checkmarks. It retains the validated dungeon map ID if the hotbar temporarily changes; unrelated puzzle maps cannot replace it. Calibration follows [NoammAddons' map padding](https://github.com/Noamm9/NoammAddons/blob/6d0c95a5e994e9acc1d043c7eb2cdac916809a31/src/main/kotlin/com/github/noamm9/utils/dungeons/map/utils/MapUtils.kt) and [physical grid](https://github.com/Noamm9/NoammAddons/blob/6d0c95a5e994e9acc1d043c7eb2cdac916809a31/src/main/kotlin/com/github/noamm9/utils/dungeons/map/handlers/DungeonScanner.kt), so a delayed player decoration cannot shift the known floor's layout. If Skyblocker's public metadata is available, its global calibration remains usable while its current-room metadata catches up to a doorway transition. Skyblocker is optional. Missing or ambiguous map data reports a waiting state.

Settings are grouped into **Route**, **Mob markers**, **Appearance** and collapsed **Advanced** controls. **Route through starred mobs** and **Route through starred minibosses** both default on and work independently: the first controls regular starred mobs, the second controls minibosses with an actual star in their label. Turning both off removes all mob visits from pathfinding and prioritizes the Blood exit; mob markers and labels remain independent. Changing either toggle updates the route immediately and does not mark mobs cleared. **Show route line** controls line visibility. **Line height** under Appearance adjusts the line from 0 to 4 blocks above the floor (default 1.2), without rebuilding geometry or moving mob markers. Lines have no arrows. The existing **Show Blood guidance** option is under Advanced and must remain on for Blood sections and the Mage doorway highlight. Existing saved keys remain under `Dungeons/Starred Clear Routing`; old feature names remain searchable. Searching for an advanced setting or opening its direct link expands Advanced. Developer diagnostics add existing graph, target, mode and timing details to `/sm route status` when Developer Mode is enabled.

With NoammAddons 1.2.9 installed, routing also reads its public scanned room/door metadata. This supplies the full topology shown by Noamm's map before those rooms appear on the native filled map. Dark undiscovered rooms and brighter discovered rooms both establish known connections; brightness never establishes completion. Only `GREEN` or `CLEARED` states count as cleared. Explicit separator tiles join multicell rooms, and explicit door tiles retain separate rooms and mandatory crossings. Loaded gate blocks take precedence over the overlay's opened flag. This integration reads metadata only, imports no room catalog or assets, and falls back to native map detection if the optional API is absent or unavailable.

Version 2.4.18 uses [NoammAddons' StarMobESP matching](https://github.com/Noamm9/NoammAddons/blob/26.1.2/src/main/kotlin/com/github/noamm9/features/impl/dungeon/StarMobESP.kt), verified against the installed 1.2.9 jar. Starred health armor stands resolve to their actual mob at label ID minus one (minus three for Withermancers), with a bounded query beneath the label when that ID is unavailable. The Shadow Assassin, Lost Adventurer, Diamond Guy and King Midas profiles identify player-shaped minibosses, but a profile alone does not establish a starred target: an actual star in the observed label is required. Unstarred minibosses are skipped. Ordinary player UUIDs and the local player are excluded from label matching. Attached nametags and direct mob names remain supported, with a 100 ms observation fallback that retries labels arriving before their body or profile. Route targets use real mob feet, not armor-stand positions. NoammAddons is not required.

Filled markers and compact distance labels appear while geometry is being scanned. Paths float at the configured line height above their validated feet positions; markers retain their 1.2-block offset; no beacon beams are drawn. Cleared markers remain optional and omit oversized text. The live mode keeps stable per-mob identities and updates meaningful movement; confirmed death clears that observed mob, whereas despawn or unload remains unknown. Only the server's room checkmark establishes whole-room completion. No unobserved spawn positions or total mob counts are fabricated. The planner handles up to twelve reachable observed mobs in a batch and retains other markers for subsequent batches.

The current-room live graph floods from a nearby supported position reachable from the player through native collision-checked connections or a checked display sight line. An airborne player can warm the scan from a valid landing without creating airborne graph nodes or certifying a walking connector. Changed geometry is published at most every 100 ms, including new edges between existing nodes. Missing destinations take priority over scanning around a moving player. Once a checked route reaches the unchanged destinations, growing snapshots keep that route until the scan finishes. While bounded approach probes are still pending, the previous target snapshot and accepted player anchor stay available; a null plan from a partial scan does not discard checked guidance. A player leaving the accepted graph or changed destinations publishes new geometry immediately. Optimization applies to available geometry. Each client tick processes at most 128 standing candidates and 256 directed connections with a two-millisecond scheduling budget. Target approach probes share a separate 64-check/two-millisecond budget. Growing snapshots retain successful and rejected approach checks, so inaccessible mobs cannot repeatedly consume the same budget before later candidates are reached. Target movement, actual geometry changes and room/world changes invalidate those caches. Current-room exit approaches accept native checked graph positions within two blocks on the same side of the door, even if the canonical anchor is decorated; the remaining crossing stays planned unless its own collision check succeeds. Rendering displays cached sections and interpolates the leading endpoint; it does not advance scans. A single native collision callback may exceed a scheduling budget. These are work limits, not a live FPS guarantee.

Carpets and `minecraft:iron_bars` are ignored in native body collision, floor support and standing-height checks, including collision-shape comparisons for repeated block updates. The underlying floor supplies the height; neither carpet nor iron bars alone can support a route across a hole. The iron-bar exception does not apply to other bars, walls or gates. The player removes obstructing iron bars manually; routing sends no mining or movement packets. Small supported player-height differences from standing on a carpet can align with the underlying floor. Slabs, snow and stair treads retain their actual collision surfaces. Native body/support sweeps validate diagonal moves and prevent cutting through corners; live costs use horizontal distance and actual elevation. Automatic graphs can add short cardinal jumps over at most two missing cells, with a maximum center-to-center distance of three blocks and level launch/landing positions. Both ends must be supported, and the full player-body corridor plus 1.25 blocks of jump headroom must be clear and loaded. These edges never introduce unsupported air nodes. Authored catalogs and explicit recording retain their adjacent-edge rules. The graph permits at most 16,384 reachable cells and does not load chunks. Target movement reuses it; relevant block and chunk changes invalidate it. Untouched areas and repeated collision-equivalent block updates do not restart the flood. Rooms at the node limit or targets outside the loaded reachable geometry report their limitation instead of inventing a route through walls.

Dungeon-level routing groups broad map seams into multicell rooms, retains narrow doors as connections between separate rooms, and searches an ordered chain toward Blood using travel estimates between actual doorway positions. Gray unopened rooms and rare rooms are usable map information; they do not establish interior walkability. Closed gates remain graph connections, while ordinary walls without a door marker never become connections. Fairy is an ordinary room in this chain, so its entry and next Wither door can appear before you enter it. These concepts adapt [NoammAddons' room/door graph](https://github.com/Noamm9/NoammAddons/blob/6d0c95a5e994e9acc1d043c7eb2cdac916809a31/src/main/kotlin/com/github/noamm9/utils/dungeons/map/handlers/DungeonTree.kt) and [map door/separator classification](https://github.com/Noamm9/NoammAddons/blob/6d0c95a5e994e9acc1d043c7eb2cdac916809a31/src/main/kotlin/com/github/noamm9/utils/dungeons/map/handlers/HotbarMapScanner.kt). The original FunnyMap was inspected as a conceptual reference; no FunnyMap code or room assets were copied.

When Blood is not yet connected, routing shows only the useful prefix shared by the confirmed forward gate frontiers. An ambiguous branch does not justify choosing an arbitrary continuation. New map information extends or revises that prefix. Loaded coal/red-terracotta gates and air/barrier observations update opening state independently of the known connection. A closed gate can be shown as a planned crossing, but it remains blocked in the native collision graph.

Room-level paths connect the player or an incoming doorway to the required outgoing doorway through actual geometry. Separate onward checks start from known door departures in loaded rooms, including Fairy, without waiting for physical entry. A clear teleport sight line between entry and a supported exit publishes two points immediately without a flood or worker job. Floor support is required at the landing, not across the gap; blocked sight lines use the bounded walking scan. Door approaches try the canonical anchor first, then supported positions within two blocks on the same side of that door. One pending room leg advances per tick with at most 64 standing candidates, 128 directed connections and a 1.5-millisecond scheduling budget. A checked onward interior publishes once its prioritized exit is connected, while the bounded flood continues; a finished scan then refines the route. There is at most one provisional and one finished-graph planning job per leg. An in-progress scan continues if the player enters its room. Completed onward suffixes are cached by room segments and entry/exit positions, reused as the player advances or the map adds more of the chain, and invalidated by relevant block/chunk changes, including adjacent collision margins. A finished blocked scan is cached until geometry changes; it does not repeat a full search while the world is unchanged. Searches use immutable snapshots on workers; publication and collision-aware simplification return to the client thread and discard stale results. No scan loads chunks.

On room entry, a previously checked interior stays available while the current graph is rebuilt when the enabled mob-stop categories have no uncleared targets, or the room is Fairy/cleared. Temporary shortening of a confirmed door chain hides its destination interior without discarding checked or queued work; relevant block/chunk changes and changed door identity still invalidate that work. A bounded native probe can supply checked teleport guidance to the first required observed starred target or the exit before the walking flood reaches it. Target approaches require a supported landing and clear sight to both the player and target; an empty or exit-only partial plan cannot suppress required-target guidance. A visible accepted anchor is retained, while completed negative probes retry after at least 0.5 blocks of player movement without restarting unfinished probes. A clear direct teleport sight line to a supported exit takes priority; otherwise a bounded collision check joins the player to the cached path. Display-only nearby joins can use a clear loaded sight line to a supported landing when the player is airborne and a walking connector fails; physical joins still require walking validation. At most seven nearby cached joins are checked. Handoffs use raw standing nodes for the same stair smoothing as normal display paths, and cache the stable suffix separately from the moving player join. Temporarily missing joins retain checked display suffixes; rendering never falls back to physical stair elbows. Final-door handoff guidance remains until the player transitions into the next room. Required observed mob stops and physical movement checks remain intact. Block packets are batched until the client tick ends so validation reads the applied world state. Obstacle removals retain cached guidance only after collision and landing revalidation; physical current-room routes also revalidate their floor support; additions, support loss and chunk changes discard them. Repeated collision-equivalent packets do not invalidate onward interiors.

Checked continuation sections use solid green lines; current starred-target legs retain the theme accent. Lines have a wider bright stroke without direction arrows, including installations with an existing thickness setting. Version 2.4.22 removes overlapping dark route strokes that covered the bright color on parts of the recorded replay. A closed planned crossing or an interior whose geometry is unavailable uses a bright dashed amber hint, with **Planned door crossing** or **Planned room connection** labels. A map-only interior hint connects known doorway anchors and may visually cross an obstacle; it is not a collision-checked walking instruction. Once that room's geometry is verified, its hint becomes a checked path around obstacles. Door centers remain mandatory crossing waypoints. A checked current-room exit path may appear before mobs are observed; that guidance does not claim the room is cleared. The persisted `showHealer` setting retains its old key but is labeled **Show Blood guidance**.

A clear teleport sight line goes straight to its required endpoint before constructing or smoothing intermediate points, including across pits. Its loaded player-sized corridor and supported landing are checked, while the intervening floor is ignored. This guides Etherwarp/AOTV use without moving the player or sending packets. Physical walking geometry and required mob stops remain unchanged. Display paths are cached separately from the physical movement route in spans of at most 64 raw edges, retaining mandatory stops. Only the partially consumed span is resmoothed as the cursor advances; later spans keep their checked curves. The short checked player connector remains separate, so sidestepping cannot drag a stair ramp across a wall. Collinear, monotone stair flights appear as straight ramps inside the already checked player-height corridor, with at most 0.6 blocks of deviation from the original treads. Level corners use small quadratic curves only when every replacement chord passes native body, support and loaded-geometry checks. Tight corners retain their original detour. Required targets, long landings, height reversals and door centers remain intact. Display smoothing never changes collision validation, planner costs, target order or the consumed route cursor; no smoothing runs during rendering.

Automatic routing uses observed mobs and the live map, rather than claiming a verified fixed spawn catalog. The optional measured templates below can supply known spawn groups before their mobs are observed. No real measured templates are bundled.

## Installing an optional verified catalog

Put the UTF-8 JSON catalog at `config/skymyce/dungeon-routing-rooms.json` in the Minecraft instance, then run `/sm route reload`. A recognized authored room supplies current-room targets and geometry when a mapped route is available; separate automatic scans handle onward rooms. Its runtime endpoint uses a supported approach to the map-selected next door. If the authored graph cannot provide that approach, current-room routing falls back to automatic geometry. `/sm route status` reports the current routing state. Reload after editing the catalog.

The author must establish the actual dungeon grid offsets and base Y coordinate from measurements. Do not substitute guessed coordinates, secret waypoints or legacy block-ID hashes for verified room clear data. The schema's legacy `healer` field stores an explicitly authored safe standing position by the intended Blood/Wither exit; its spelling is retained for catalog compatibility.

## Recording a room in Minecraft

Recording is an explicit local authoring workflow. Measure the room's canonical origin, total spawn-group counts and intended Blood/Wither exit first. Start inside an active, non-boss dungeon room:

1. Stand at the measured origin block and run `/sm route record start <room-id> <quarterTurns>`. Quarter turns are `0`–`3`, following the table below. A new catalog uses the origin's X/Z modulo 32 and block Y for its grid. With an installed catalog, the selected X/Z must already align with that grid; its existing origin Y is retained.
2. Aim at two opposing structural corner blocks and run `/sm route record corner` for each. Select the room's lower floor/wall extent and its opposing upper wall/ceiling extent. Bounds must include all intended feet cells **and every landmark block**, including upper levels; player feet alone do not describe structural bounds. Each axis spans at most 128 cells, coordinates remain within local ±128, and the scan volume is at most 65,536 candidate cells. Use tight bounds; the final room permits at most 16,384 walkable cells.
3. Look directly at a stable structural block and run `/sm route record landmark`; repeat for at least three distinctive blocks noncollinear in X/Z. Air, fluids and blocks without collision are rejected. Landmarks must lie inside the recorded bounds and identify exactly one room orientation.
4. Stand at each verified starred spawn group's safe approach and run `/sm route record target <target-id> <count>`. Supply the verified total number of starred mobs in that group, from 1 to 128. There may be at most 12 groups. Overlapping or ambiguous spawn regions cannot establish reliable mob associations.
5. Stand at the intended Blood/Wither-door approach and run `/sm route record healer` (the legacy command name). Targets and exit positions use integer candidate feet heights; fractional slab/stair heights round up and must pass native support/collision checks.
6. Open the intended passages, then run `/sm route record scan`. The existing incremental builder examines at most 128 candidate cells and 256 directed connections per tick, reads only loaded chunks, and derives neighbor candidates only after standing cells pass collision/support checks. Cells blocked during capture are omitted from the template; later runtime checks can close an authored passage but cannot invent an omitted one. Keep the room and its chunk margin loaded. `/sm route record status` reports progress or the rejection reason.
7. When the scan is complete, run `/sm route record save`. The full merged catalog is validated before an atomic local write; the original catalog is backed up as `dungeon-routing-rooms.json.before-room-recording` on the first save. Existing room IDs, incompatible grids and malformed existing files are rejected. Success reloads the routing catalog automatically.

Editing a draft or changing relevant blocks/chunks invalidates its scan; run `/scan` again before `/save`. `/sm route record cancel` discards the draft. Death, a dungeon reset or a changed world cancels unfinished recording. A save already explicitly requested may finish after cancellation. Recording adds only the current floor: add another floor label only after validating its layout, spawn counts and endpoint. Reuse one canonical template for its four rotations.

The recorded geometry is a collision-checked snapshot, not proof of production room support. Validate recognition, all rotations and real target lifecycle before distributing a catalog. Runtime revalidates authored cells and connections against the current loaded world.

## Schema 1

Every field below except target `count` is required. This is a **synthetic format example**, not a Hypixel room or a usable production catalog:

```json
{
  "schema": 1,
  "grid": { "spacing": 32, "offsetX": 0, "offsetZ": 0, "originY": 0 },
  "rooms": [{
    "id": "synthetic-only",
    "floors": ["F1"],
    "bounds": { "min": [0, 0, 0], "max": [2, 1, 2] },
    "landmarks": [
      { "cell": [0, 1, 2], "block": "minecraft:stone" },
      { "cell": [1, 1, 2], "block": "minecraft:andesite" },
      { "cell": [0, 1, 1], "block": "minecraft:sandstone" }
    ],
    "walkable": [[0, 0, 0], [1, 0, 0], [2, 0, 0], [2, 0, 1], [2, 0, 2]],
    "edges": [
      [[0, 0, 0], [1, 0, 0]],
      [[1, 0, 0], [2, 0, 0]],
      [[2, 0, 0], [2, 0, 1]],
      [[2, 0, 1], [2, 0, 2]]
    ],
    "targets": [{ "id": "star-a", "cell": [1, 0, 0], "count": 2 }],
    "healer": [2, 0, 2]
  }]
}
```

| Field | Meaning and validation |
| --- | --- |
| `schema` | Integer `1`. |
| `grid` | `spacing` must be `32`. Offsets are signed world X/Z integers within ±29,999,000; `originY` is within ±4,096. Candidate origins are `(offsetX + 32*i, originY, offsetZ + 32*j)`. |
| `rooms` | At most 256 templates. `[]` is a valid empty catalog. |
| `id` | Unique stable room ID matching `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_.-]{0,63}`. |
| `floors` | Nonempty, distinct labels from `E`, `F1`–`F7`, `M1`–`M7`. Only list floors actually validated for the template. |
| `bounds` | Inclusive local cell bounds. Each coordinate is within ±128; each axis spans at most 128 cells. |
| `landmarks` | 3–64 distinct cells in bounds, including three noncollinear positions in X/Z. Block IDs use a namespaced resource identifier, at most 128 characters; block states are not included. Choose stable, distinctive structural blocks. |
| `walkable` | 1–16,384 distinct local feet cells in bounds. At most 131,072 cells across the catalog. These are candidate standing positions, not a declaration that the client may ignore collisions. |
| `edges` | At most 65,536 distinct undirected pairs of walkable cells. X/Z Manhattan difference is at most one, Y difference at most one, and endpoints must differ. Diagonals, jumps over missing cells and duplicate/reversed pairs are rejected. |
| `targets` | At most 12 targets, each with a unique ID using the same pattern as room IDs and a `cell` in `walkable`. Optional `count` is the verified starred mob total for that spawn group, from 1 to 128. An omitted count remains unknown and cannot establish completion. Use confirmed starred spawn groups, not ordinary mobs or secrets. An empty list permits a healer-only route. |
| `healer` | Legacy field name for a measured walkable feet cell at the intended safe Blood/Wither-door approach. The field remains required for catalog compatibility; runtime guidance uses the map-selected door approach. Closed doors remain physically blocked. |

All coordinates and numeric fields are plain signed 32-bit JSON integer literals. Numeric strings, decimals, exponent notation, duplicate property names, invalid UTF-8 and trailing content are rejected. Input is limited to 4 MiB and nesting depth 16. The entire catalog is rejected on malformed data; partially valid rooms are not silently installed.

## Coordinates and orientation

Cells are integer block indices; Y is the player's feet height. The template origin is local `[0,0,0]`. Positive quarter turns map local +X to world +Z:

| Quarter turns | Local `(x,z)` becomes |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | `(x,z)` |
| 1 | `(-z,x)` |
| 2 | `(-x,-z)` |
| 3 | `(z,-x)` |

Add the world origin after rotating integer cell coordinates. For rendering or standing positions, **transform the cell first**, then add `0.5` to world X/Z; retain world Y as feet height. This rotates block centers about the origin block's center. Do not rotate already-centered local points with the raw vector `RoomPoint` overload and assume the result is a transformed block center.

Recognition floors the player's world position to a block cell, considers only matching floor templates and nearby grid origins within four tiles, then checks transformed landmarks. A candidate also needs an authored walkable feet cell within two blocks of the player; the bounded search covers X/Z ±2 and Y ±1. This rejects another room occupying the empty quadrant of an L-shaped room's rectangular bounds. Live geometry independently validates the actual player-to-node connector. Landmark reads are memoized and capped at 8,192 distinct blocks per recognition. Unloaded blocks never count as air. More than one matching template, origin or orientation suppresses the route rather than selecting an arbitrary match.

## Authoring and validation

1. Record a room's actual grid origin, orientation and complete bounds. Convert measurements into one canonical local orientation; do not add separate templates for rotated copies.
2. Select at least three distinctive structural landmarks spread across X/Z. Check that they identify exactly one orientation and do not depend on mob entities, loot, opened doors or changing blocks.
3. Record safe feet cells and every intended connection. Include actual corridors, stairs and climbable sections. Never join nodes across a wall, unsupported hole or blocked doorway merely because their endpoints are close.
4. Record fixed starred spawn locations and the intended healer approach. Validate which floors and spawn groups share the layout. Each spawn group needs a safe reachable walkable node outside solid geometry.
5. Test all four rotations, multiple grid origins and arbitrary player starts, including the room center. Test partially cleared rooms, inaccessible targets, closed doors, missing chunks and changed geometry before claiming room support.

Minecraft world reads stay on the client thread. Geometry builders check loaded chunk margins, a 0.6-wide/1.8-high player body, support, swept connectors, conservative one-block ascents and climbable vertical connectors. Small same-column adjustments up to 0.6 blocks may align actual slab feet with an integer candidate node only when the entire swept body is clear and every vertical sample stays supported or climbable; full-block vertical rises still require a ladder. Horizontal support probes shrink by half their sampling interval so a narrow unsupported hole cannot hide between samples. No builder loads chunks. Authored validation, recording and onward interior scans publish completed snapshots; automatic current-room scans can publish partial checked geometry. In authored mode, a node or edge absent from the template is never invented; live collision checks may remove authored nodes and edges. Automatic routing additionally considers the bounded level cardinal gap jumps described above; explicit recording still derives adjacent neighbors. Every physical candidate passes native movement checks. Block/chunk invalidation uses the shared collision envelope, including support and jump-headroom margins.

## Routing and target state

`RoomNavigation` caches Dijkstra paths from fixed targets. Each replan searches from the player's actual reachable node, then uses Held–Karp dynamic programming to minimize the complete path through remaining targets **including the selected door approach**. Cost sums horizontal and vertical movement blocks along validated edges: a horizontal step costs one and a one-block ascent with horizontal movement costs two. Equal-distance paths prefer fewer direction changes without accepting a longer detour. It is not a time prediction or straight-line distance. Up to 12 targets keeps exact ordering bounded. Collision/support-aware line-of-sight simplification removes redundant level waypoints after pathfinding; separate legs retain required targets and door crossings, and elevation changes retain stair/jump elbows.

A still-valid previous target order or dungeon door chain is retained unless a replacement saves more than three movement blocks. Map context refreshes every 100 ms. Walking along a valid checked route suffix updates only the leading connector and cursor; it does not trigger another search. A meaningful deviation requests a replan after 100 ms when automatic recalculation is enabled; target, room, geometry and teleport changes request it immediately. Stationary observations reuse the previous result, and rendering interpolates the leading endpoint. New worker graphs can start planning immediately. Ordinary walking or jumping off the previous connector retains its checked remaining path while awaiting a replacement; actual teleports and invalid geometry hide stale guidance. A failed current-room plan has one bounded retry after 500 ms. The connector may advance only within the first target leg and must pass collision/support checks, so it cannot skip a still-needed target or snap through a wall.

`RoomTargetStates` distinguishes `UNKNOWN`, `ACTIVE` and `CLEARED`. Only confirmed deaths complete targets, and the confirmed count must equal the group's authored `count`. Missing counts, incomplete sightings, excess observations and conflicting associations fail to unknown. Disappearance or chunk unload becomes unknown; an active observed member keeps its group unfinished. Confirmed completion survives temporary unloads and player death within the same run. Entity-to-target associations remain stable as mobs move or unload. Reaching the mapped approach after clearing all targets finishes the local target leg only when the player is nearby **and a safe connector reaches the endpoint**; proximity through a wall cannot finish the room. Onward door/room guidance remains available. A newly observed mob can reactivate the local leg. A run reset clears observations and completion. The 128-observation limit per target fails to unknown rather than claiming completion.

## Developer checks

Use Java 25:

```powershell
.\gradlew.bat dungeonRoutingCheck
.\gradlew.bat check build
```

`dungeonRoutingCheck` runs `roomNavigationCheck`, `roomTemplatesCheck`, `roomWorldGeometryCheck`, `roomRoutingUpdatesCheck` and `dungeonRouteContinuationCheck`. Checks cover fixed-grid floor calibration, displaced entrances and stale map markers, half-block room boundaries, routing until Blood opens, Mage-only run-long doorway reminders hidden by physical Blood containment, independent starred-mob/miniboss routing, gray/rare rooms, multicell and L-shaped containment, ordered multi-door topology toward Blood, pre-entry Fairy continuation, partial frontier extension, closed-door approaches, wall detours, equal-distance turn preference, line-of-sight clearance and preserved stair/target waypoints, exact target order, live flood budgets and caps, immutable snapshots, unloaded cells, collision/support geometry, authored catalogs and target lifecycle. Display checks cover ascending/descending ramps, repeated treads, landings, reversals, safe curves, walls and unavailable geometry. Teleport sight checks allow unsupported gaps and airborne starts while requiring a valid destination, clear loaded body corridor and bounded range. Physical gap-jump checks require supported launch/landing positions and reject excessive spans, diagonal jumps, blocked headroom and unloaded geometry. Carpet/iron-bar fixtures cover ignored collision shapes, underlying floors and missing support. Continuation checks exercise early checked publication and final refinement, entry during scanning, blocked anchor fallback, cached Fairy handoff, safe nearby airborne display joins with blocked final sight, retained interiors through temporary chain prefixes, raw display versus physical stair paths, cached suffix reuse, queued worker jobs and rejection of stale results after block/chunk changes. They do not establish every real floor's coverage or live rendering/FPS behavior, and do not replace Minecraft validation. Developer diagnostics should remain disabled during normal play and record room identity, orientation, origin, targets, endpoint, geometry status, route cost/order, replan reason and execution time only when requested.

### Reproducible in-game checks

1. Enable routing and enter the waiting room with Noamm's full scanned map available. Confirm known guidance appears before the start announcement and stays visible when Mort starts the run. With a known onward route, stop before a closed Wither door: confirm later door crossings remain visible, the closed crossing is dashed amber, and opening it changes crossing validation without revealing the entire route anew.
2. Before entering Fairy, inspect its entry and next Wither door. Once its loaded interior scan finishes, confirm the solid green path enters the opening and goes around the tree or other obstacles. Enter Fairy and verify the same onward suffix is retained.
3. Start with incomplete map information, then discover more rooms/doors. Confirm the unambiguous prefix extends toward Blood and no arbitrary route appears at an unresolved branch. Observe dashed interiors becoming solid only after geometry validation.
4. Follow the checked route, move slightly, then leave it meaningfully or change a relevant block/chunk. Confirm ordinary movement remains stable, relevant changes recalculate, smoothing preserves door centers and stairs, and solid sections never cross walls. Clear teleport guidance may cross gaps; walking detours remain where sight is blocked.
5. Have another player open Blood while you are elsewhere. Confirm route lines, route labels and mob markers immediately hide and stay hidden when leaving Blood. As Mage, confirm the actual Blood doorway is highlighted before opening and remains highlighted outside Blood for the run, including after more than 23 seconds.
